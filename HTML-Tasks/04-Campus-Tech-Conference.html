About the Event

The TechNova Campus Summit 2026 is a one-day technology conference designed for students who want to understand how modern software systems are built, deployed, secured, and maintained.

The event brings together software developers, cybersecurity professionals, cloud engineers, and technology educators for a full day of technical sessions, practical demonstrations, and discussions.

The conference is primarily intended for undergraduate students, but students from other academic backgrounds who are interested in technology are also welcome.

The event begins at 9:00 AM and ends at 6:00 PM on October 17, 2026.

Featured Speakers

Dr. Ananya Sen

Role: Principal Software Engineer

Company: CloudSphere Technologies

Session: Designing Scalable Web Applications

Dr. Ananya Sen has more than ten years of experience designing and developing large-scale web applications.

Her session will focus on how engineers make architectural decisions when applications grow from a few hundred users to millions of users.

Rahul Kapoor

Role: Cybersecurity Engineer

Company: SecureNet Labs

Session: Thinking Like an Attacker

Rahul Kapoor works in application security and penetration testing.

His session will introduce students to common web security weaknesses and explain how developers can design applications that are harder to attack.

Priya Nair

Role: Cloud Infrastructure Engineer

Company: DataGrid Systems

Session: From Localhost to the Cloud

Priya Nair specializes in cloud infrastructure and deployment automation.

Her session will explain how a project moves from a developer's laptop to a production environment.

Opening Keynote

Designing Technology for Real Users

Speaker: Dr. Ananya Sen

Time: 9:30 AM - 10:30 AM

Modern software is not successful simply because it works. It also needs to be reliable, maintainable, accessible, and capable of serving its users efficiently.

This keynote explores the engineering decisions involved in building software that remains useful as its number of users, features, and dependencies increases.

"Good engineering is not about making software complicated. It is about making complexity manageable."

-Dr. Ananya Sen

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions
