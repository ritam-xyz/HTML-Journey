❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join DevConnect?

You can create a free account through our registration page and join our community channels.


Can beginners participate?

Yes, all developers of any skill level are welcome to learn, build, and ask questions.


Can I submit my own project?

Yes, members can showcase their open-source projects in the Featured Projects registry.


How do I contribute to open source?

Browse our tagged beginner-friendly issues or participate in our community sprints.


Are DevConnect events free?

Yes, all standard workshops, sprints, and meetups are completely free of charge.


Can I become a community mentor?

Experienced developers can apply to be mentors through our community support desk.
